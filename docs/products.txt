==================================================
OVERVIEW
==================================================

TechLogic S.p.A. designs and manufactures precision CNC-turned metallic components for industrial, mechanical, and instrumentation applications. The product range is divided into three main lines, each optimised for a different customer need: micro-scale fastening and hardware, precision housings for optical and sensing systems, and pre-validated ready-to-ship stock components. Custom orders based on customer drawings are accepted across all product families.

==================================================
PRODUCT LINE 1 — MICRO TURNED FASTENERS
==================================================

Category: Precision Hardware and Miniature Mechanical Components
Primary markets: electronics manufacturing, automotive sensors, medical devices, scientific instrumentation, watchmaking, pneumatic micro-assemblies

Description:
The Micro Turned Fasteners line covers the full spectrum of miniature and small-format turned hardware used in compact industrial assemblies. Parts in this family are produced on high-speed Swiss-type automatic turning centres, which maintain dimensional stability at small diameters through guide-bushing support of the bar stock. Every fastener in this line is produced with full batch traceability linked to the originating material heat number.

Product sub-families:

1.1 Miniature Machine Screws
   Standard head forms: pan head, countersunk (flat head), cylindrical head, hexagon socket head
   Thread range: M0.8 × 0.2 through M10 × 1.5; UNC #0-80 through 3/8"-16 available on request
   Diameter range: 0.8 mm to 10 mm shank
   Length range: 1.5 mm to 60 mm
   Standard tolerance: ISO 6g for external thread; IT7 on shank diameter
   Materials: stainless 303, stainless 316L, brass CuZn39Pb3, aluminium 6061-T6, carbon steel 11SMnPb37
   Surface options: plain, passivated (stainless), nickel plated, zinc plated (white/yellow/black)
   Typical applications: PCB standoffs, sensor mounting, miniature enclosure fastening

1.2 Threaded Inserts and Bushings
   Types: self-tapping inserts, press-fit bushings, flanged bushings, step bushings, guide pins
   OD range: 2 mm to 20 mm
   Length range: 2 mm to 35 mm
   Thread internal bore: M1.6 to M8; smooth bore also available
   Tolerance: IT6 on OD for press-fit variants; H7 on internal bore standard
   Materials: brass CuZn39Pb3, stainless 303, steel 11SMnPb37, aluminium 6061
   Surface options: plain, nickel plated, hard anodised (aluminium variants)
   Typical applications: plastic assembly inserts, motor and bearing housings, fixture alignment

1.3 Precision Dowel and Taper Pins
   Diameter range: 1 mm to 12 mm
   Length range: 5 mm to 80 mm
   Cylindricity tolerance: ≤ 0.003 mm on ground variants
   Surface roughness: Ra ≤ 0.4 µm (ground finish option available)
   Materials: stainless 303, tool steel, aluminium
   Taper options: 1:50 standard (ISO 2339); custom tapers on request
   Typical applications: precision fixture dowelling, assembly alignment, shear pin applications

1.4 Custom Micro-Turned Parts (fastener family)
   Produced to customer drawing. Geometry feasibility review included in quotation process. Typical lead time: 10–15 working days.

PRODUCT LINE 2 — TELEMETRY SENSOR BODIES
==================================================

Category: Precision Machined Housings and Instrumentation Components
Primary markets: industrial metrology, quality control equipment, laser measurement systems, non-destructive testing (NDT), autonomous inspection platforms

Description:
The Telemetry Sensor Bodies line provides machined housings, mounting brackets, and calibration-ready mounts specifically engineered for optical and sensing systems used in laser-vision quality control lines. Parts in this family require tight cylindricity, co-axiality, and low surface roughness to avoid optical path distortion or sensor signal drift. Production is performed on precision turning centres with live tooling and is followed by CMM validation of all form tolerances.

Product sub-families:

2.1 Sensor Housing Cylinders
   OD range: 8 mm to 80 mm
   Bore diameter: 4 mm to 60 mm
   Wall thickness: minimum 1.5 mm
   Length: 10 mm to 150 mm
   Cylindricity: ≤ 0.005 mm standard; ≤ 0.002 mm on request
   Co-axiality of bore to OD: ≤ 0.008 mm standard
   Surface roughness (bore): Ra ≤ 0.8 µm standard; Ra ≤ 0.4 µm on request
   Materials: aluminium 6061-T6, aluminium 7075-T6, stainless 316L, titanium grade 5 (Ti6Al4V)
   Connector styles: M12×0.5 thread-in, bayonet, flanged clamp, custom on request
   Typical applications: laser triangulation sensor bodies, confocal displacement probe housings, fibre-optic collimator mounts

2.2 Calibration Mounts and Alignment Brackets
   Precision datum surfaces with parallelism and perpendicularity tolerances ≤ 0.005 mm
   Adjustable angle variants available (fixed or slotted)
   Through-holes with H7 tolerance for pin or bolt alignment
   Materials: aluminium 6061 (standard), stainless 303 (high-stability variant)
   Surface treatment: hard anodise for aluminium mounts; passivation for stainless
   Typical applications: mounting blocks for laser line scanners, vision camera brackets, CMM probe holders

2.3 Custom Sensor Bodies and Instrument Housings
   Produced to customer drawing or 3D model (STEP/IGES). Complete DFM (design for manufacturability) review included. Typical lead time: 12–18 working days for first article.

MATERIALS REFERENCE TABLE
==================================================

Aluminium 6061-T6:       General machining, brackets, sensor bodies. Good corrosion resistance, excellent machinability.
Aluminium 7075-T6:       High-strength structural components. Higher hardness; slightly reduced machinability vs 6061.
Stainless Steel 303:     Free-machining stainless for fasteners and hardware. Not suitable for welding applications.
Stainless Steel 304:     Standard austenitic stainless. Better corrosion resistance; slightly harder to machine than 303.
Stainless Steel 316L:    Marine and chemical environments. Higher molybdenum content; excellent pitting resistance.
Brass CuZn39Pb3:         Excellent machinability, good conductivity, low friction. Suitable for inserts, fittings, bushings.
Carbon Steel 11SMnPb37:  Free-machining carbon steel for non-corrosive environments. Low cost, high volume production.
Titanium Grade 2:        Commercially pure Ti. High corrosion resistance, lower strength than Ti6Al4V.
Titanium Grade 5 (Ti6Al4V): Aerospace and medical grade. High strength-to-weight. Longer cycle times; premium pricing.
PEEK:                    Engineering plastic for lightweight, low-friction, or electrically isolating components.

ORDERING CODES AND PART NUMBERING
==================================================

TechLogic assigns a unique part number to every custom component upon first quotation. The format is:
  TL-[ProductFamily]-[DrawingRevision]-[CustomerCode]-[Sequence]

Example: TL-MF-03-CUST001-0047
  TL = TechLogic
  MF = Micro Fasteners family
  03 = drawing revision 3
  CUST001 = customer code
  0047 = sequential component number

Stock-line components use a catalogue prefix:
  TL-STK-[Material Code]-[Dimension Code]

Example: TL-STK-SS303-M3X8-CSH refers to a stainless 303 M3×0.5 countersunk hex socket head screw, 8 mm length.

Customers are encouraged to reference TechLogic part numbers on all purchase orders to eliminate drawing-revision ambiguity.
