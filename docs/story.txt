# COMPANY HISTORY AND VALUES
**Keywords:** company history, Eraclio Capitani, Michele Capitani, generational transition, sustainability

## Our Heritage
For over 50 years, Capitani has been a benchmark in the production of high-precision metal parts. The small metal parts company was founded by Eraclio Capitani as a small shop operating a few traditional cam lathes, the company has grown into a highly automated, Industry 4.0 compliant facility. Today, the company is led by his son, Michele Capitani, successfully navigating the generational transition while maintaining a long-term vision for the business.

## Quality and Sustainability
We believe the future of manufacturing lies in sustainable practices. We are ISO 9001:2015 certified, ensuring rigorous quality management. Recently, we published our 2024 Sustainability Report, highlighting our commitment to the environment. Our facility is powered by over 6,300 square feet (590 sq. meters) of solar panels, and we utilize closed-loop vacuum washers and advanced oil-separation and chip-centrifuge systems to minimize our ecological footprint.
