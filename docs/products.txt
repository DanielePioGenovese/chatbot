# PRODUCT CATALOG AND CAPABILITIES
**Keywords:** custom metal parts, turned parts, surface treatments, manufacturing sectors, CNC machining

## Core Offerings
Capitani Metal Parts specializes in precision turned metal components manufactured entirely to customer drawings and specifications. Thanks to our deep knowledge of materials and production processes, our technical office works alongside our clients to solve complex engineering and manufacturing challenges. 

## Supported Sectors
We manufacture customized components for a wide variety of industries, including:
*   Railway & Mass Transit
*   Automotive & Transportation
*   Electronics & Telecommunications
*   Plumbing, HVAC, and Sanitary
*   Electrical Power & Distribution
*   Hydraulics (Oleodinamica)
*   Agriculture Machinery
*   Lighting Fixtures

## Surface Finishes and Treatments
Through established partnerships with certified external vendors, we deliver fully finished products. Available surface treatments include:
*   Zinc plating & Zinc-nickel plating
*   Nickel plating & Chrome plating
*   Blackening (Black Oxide)
*   Copper plating & Tin plating
*   Geomet® coatings
*   Annealing, Case-hardening, Nitriding, and Tempering
*   Phosphating & Passivation
*   Anodizing (Anodic oxidation) & Alodine
