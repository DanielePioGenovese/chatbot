# MANUFACTURING PROCESS AND QUALITY ASSURANCE
**Source:** https://www.capitanimetalparts.com/en/process
**Keywords:** traceability, workflow, inspection, certification, compliance

## 100% Traceability System
Total traceability is the foundation of our production cycle. All raw materials arrive with official mill certifications. Upon entering the facility, materials are inspected, color-coded, and paired with an internal "Identity Document." This digital and physical ID follows the specific batch through every step of the workflow—from raw bar stock to the final delivered product.

## Inspection Protocols
Quality is not just checked at the end; it is built into the process. We have implemented accurate verification processes both on-board the machine during production runs and at the end of the cycle. All our measuring instruments and gauges are uniquely coded, revised, and officially recalibrated and certified annually to ensure absolute compliance with client drawings.
